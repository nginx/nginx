Upstream: fixed rounding of least_time average response times.

Average times used by the least_time balancer were kept in whole
milliseconds and updated as avg = 0.5 + v * 0.05 + avg * 0.95, then
truncated.  A sample changed the average only if it differed from it
by at least 10ms, so the average could stay anywhere within 10ms of
the actual time.  In particular, the average of a server responding
in less than 10ms could only grow, on occasional slow responses, and
never decreased again: such averages converged to about 10ms
regardless of the actual response time.

Averages are now kept in 1/64ms units and converted back to
milliseconds when computing the estimated time, before the 5000ms
limit and the 20ms clustering are applied.  Values are limited to
NGX_MAX_INT32_VALUE to avoid overflow on 32-bit platforms.
